
The transport layer plays a crucial role in facilitationg communication betwenen two devices across a network. This layer ensures reliable, end-to-end communication.

 TCP (Transmission Control Protocol): Connection-oriented protocol providing reliable and ordered delivery of data.

TCP three-way handshake is a process used to establish a reliable connection between two devices before they begin data transmission.


UDP (User Datagram Protocol): Connectionless protocol that is faster but provides no guarentees regarding the order or reliability of data delivery.
